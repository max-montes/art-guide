## False-exact analysis (3 cases)

These are the most important calibration signal. A "false exact" is when:
- `returned_status = exact`
- `recall_at_1 = False` (wrong artwork ID)
- `status_match = True` (expected status was also "exact")

| Expected artwork | Returned artwork | Confidence |
|-----------------|-----------------|------------|
| met:435877 | met:436535 | 0.853 |
| met:435876 | met:436529 | 0.851 |
| met:435839 | met:436597 | **0.926** ← highest |

The 0.926 case is notable. A 19th-century European painting (likely Cézanne or El Greco) scored 0.926 cosine similarity against a Van Gogh — crossing the "exact" threshold. In a 100-record catalog all from the same period and museum, the embedding space is too compressed to distinguish between similar artworks confidently.

**Expected behavior post-full-ingest:** The 500K catalog introduces a wide variety of styles, periods, and media. The delta between a true match and a nearest neighbor grows; false-exact rate should drop significantly.
